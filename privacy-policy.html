What we collect

When you fill out a form on this site, we collect the details you give us, such as your name, email address, phone number, property address and message. We also record the page you submitted from and, if you arrived from an ad or link, the campaign information attached to it.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your request, provide real estate services and follow up about your inquiry. We do not sell your personal information.

Who helps us

We use service providers to run the site and store your information, including a hosted database provider and, if enabled, analytics and advertising tools such as Google Analytics and Meta Pixel. These providers process data on our behalf.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information as long as it is useful for serving you or required by law, then delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we have about you, or to stop contacting you, by emailing us at the address on our contact page.
