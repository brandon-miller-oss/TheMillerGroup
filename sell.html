Selling your home

From the first price conversation to the closing table, here’s how we work.

How a sale works

  1. Set the price

    We review recent comparable sales and the current competition to set a price that brings in offers.

  2. Get it ready

    A few targeted repairs, a deep clean and some staging advice go a long way.

  3. Market it

    Photos, a listing that tells the story of the home, online exposure and showings.

  4. Negotiate

    We compare each offer on price, terms, timing and the buyer’s financing.

  5. Close

    We manage the inspection, appraisal and title steps so you can plan your move.

Common questions

How do you decide on a price?

We compare your home with recent sales nearby, look at what’s currently for sale, and talk about your timeline. Then we agree on a price together.

What does it cost to sell?

Typical costs include agent compensation, title and closing costs, and any repairs or concessions you agree to. We give you an estimate of your net proceeds before you list.

Should I make repairs first?

Sometimes. We’ll walk through the home with you and tell you which fixes are worth the money and which aren’t.

How long will it take to sell?

It depends on price, condition and the season. We’ll share what homes like yours have been doing in your area so you can plan.
