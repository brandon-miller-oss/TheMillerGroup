Equal housing opportunity

The Miller Group supports the Fair Housing Act and Colorado fair housing law. We do not discriminate on any basis protected by federal, state or local law, including race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status and national origin, along with the additional protections that apply in Colorado.

If you believe you have experienced housing discrimination, you can contact the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development or the Colorado Civil Rights Division.
