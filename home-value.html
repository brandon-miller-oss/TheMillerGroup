What’s my home worth?

Tell us about your home and we’ll send you a personal estimate based on recent nearby sales. It’s free, and there’s no pressure to list.

How the estimate works

We look at homes similar to yours that sold recently nearby, then adjust for size, condition, updates and location. You’ll get a price range and a short explanation.

This is our opinion of value, not a formal appraisal. If you need an appraisal for a loan or legal purpose, you’ll need a licensed appraiser.
