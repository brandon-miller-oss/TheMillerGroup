Buying a home in Colorado

Condo in the city, cabin in the mountains, or a place with some land. We start by asking how you spend your weekends.

  • We look at the neighborhood and your daily routine along with the house itself
  • Offer strategy for competitive listings and for slower markets
  • Out-of-state and second-home buyers are welcome. We handle showings and logistics remotely.

Selling your home

Most of a good sale comes down to price, presentation, and keeping a level head when the inspection report comes back.

  • Pricing based on recent comparable sales and what buyers are doing right now
  • A marketing plan built around what you love about the house
  • Support through inspection, appraisal and closing

Commercial space

We work with office, retail, flex and industrial space, including properties with a yard or outdoor storage.

  • Tenants: we find options, tour them with you, and negotiate the lease
  • Landlords and owners: pricing, marketing and finding the right tenant
  • Buyers and investors: help with owner-user purchases and investment properties

Land and development

If you own a parcel and wonder what it could become, or you’re looking for a site to build on, we can help you work through it.

  • Early site screening: zoning, access, utilities and floodplain
  • Feasibility and pro forma help, including affordable housing projects
  • Introductions to builders, buyers and capital partners

      Thinking about development?

      Colorado keeps growing, and every parcel raises the same question: what could go here, and do the numbers work? Here’s how we help you find out.

      1. Screen the site

        We check zoning, access, utilities, floodplain and market demand early, so you find the problems before you buy.

      2. Test the numbers

        We help build the feasibility study and pro forma for market-rate, mixed-use and affordable housing projects.

      3. Line up the next steps

        We introduce you to builders, buyers and capital, and stay with you through contracts, due diligence and closing.
