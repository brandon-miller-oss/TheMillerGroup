Last updated November 2026.

Our commitment

We want everyone to be able to use this website. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 level AA and keep improving as we go.

Tell us if something isn’t working

If you have trouble using any part of this site, or need information in another format, please contact us using the details on our contact page and we will work with you.
