Buying a home in Colorado

Here’s how the process works, what to expect along the way, and where we can help.

The process, step by step

  1. Get pre-approved

    Talk to a lender before you tour. A pre-approval tells you what you can spend and makes your offer stronger.

  2. Decide what matters

    Commute, yard, HOA fees, mountain access. We’ll sort the must-haves from the nice-to-haves.

  3. Tour homes

    We set up the showings and tell you what we notice, good and bad.

  4. Make an offer

    We look at recent sales, the condition of the home and the seller’s situation to build an offer that fits.

  5. Inspect and appraise

    You’ll have a window to inspect the home and negotiate repairs. Your lender orders the appraisal.

  6. Close

    We review the final numbers, do a last walkthrough and hand you the keys.

Common questions

How much do I need for a down payment?

It depends on the loan. Some conventional loans start at 3 to 5 percent down, FHA loans can start at 3.5 percent, and VA loans can require nothing down for eligible buyers. A lender can tell you what you qualify for.

Should I get pre-approved before I start looking?

Yes. A pre-approval shows you your real budget, and sellers take your offer more seriously when a lender has already reviewed your finances.

Can you help if I live out of state?

Yes. We handle video tours, showings and paperwork remotely. Many of our buyers are relocating or buying a second home from out of state.

How are buyer agents paid?

Compensation is set in a written agreement before we start working together. We go over it with you up front so there are no surprises.
